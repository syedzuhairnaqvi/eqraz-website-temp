Why halal financing matters

Avoiding riba is not just a responsibility, it is also an opportunity.

A divine command.

Riba is explicitly prohibited in the Qur'an and Sunnah. For a believer, avoiding it is not a preference but an obligation — and EQRAZ exists to make obeying it possible, without compromise.

The harm of riba.

Interest-based finance concentrates wealth and deepens injustice on a global scale. Choosing halal is choosing a fairer system.

A stronger community.

Every halal mortgage strengthens Canada's Muslim financial sphere and the community's collective voice. Your choice builds something bigger than a home.

A structure that respects Canadian tax rules.

How it works

How Monthly Murabaha (Tawarruq-based) financing works.

EQRAZ's financing is built on Shariah-compliant purchase-and-sale transactions — an alternative to interest-bearing lending. In simple steps: you choose your home; the financing is generated through real commodity transactions completed in the required sequence (Tawarruq); you take sole legal and beneficial ownership of your home from closing; and you repay through predictable monthly payments made up of principal and a fixed, known profit — never interest. The step-by-step process is explained in plain language on How Financing Works.

Independent servicing and custody

Professional administration, independent of EQRAZ.

After closing, day-to-day mortgage administration — including payments, statements and customer servicing — is handled by an independent professional mortgage administrator. A leading Canadian custodian performs the applicable custody role under the financing structure. These independent arrangements reduce reliance on EQRAZ alone and are designed to support continuity, professional administration and ongoing Shariah governance if EQRAZ's circumstances change. You remain the sole legal and beneficial owner of your home.
